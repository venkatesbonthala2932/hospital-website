ENT Specialists

Our world-class otolaryngologists provide comprehensive care for conditions affecting the ear, nose, throat, and related structures of the head and neck. We combine advanced diagnostic technology with compassionate expertise.

Dr. Rajesh Kumar

MBBS, MS (ENT), Fellowship in Head & Neck Oncology

work_history 18 Years Experience

Dr. Kumar specializes in complex head and neck surgeries and advanced sinus procedures. He is renowned for his meticulous surgical technique and patient-centered approach to chronic ENT conditions.

Dr. Ananya Sharma

MBBS, DNB (ENT)

work_history 12 Years Experience

An expert in pediatric otolaryngology, Dr. Sharma brings a gentle, empathetic approach to treating children with ear infections, tonsil issues, and airway disorders. She is dedicated to family-inclusive care.

Dr. Vikram Singh

MBBS, MS (ENT), FRCS

work_history 25 Years Experience

Dr. Singh is a pioneer in endoscopic sinus surgery and cochlear implants. His decades of experience and commitment to incorporating the latest technological advancements ensure superior outcomes for complex auditory issues.

Dr. Meera Patel

MBBS, MS (ENT), Fellowship in Laryngology

work_history 9 Years Experience

Focusing on voice and swallowing disorders, Dr. Patel utilizes state-of-the-art stroboscopy and laser treatments. She is highly sought after by professional voice users for her specialized laryngology expertise.

Dr. Sanjay Gupta

MBBS, MS (ENT)

work_history 15 Years Experience

Dr. Gupta specializes in general otolaryngology with a particular interest in allergy management and sleep apnea. He focuses on non-invasive therapies and holistic treatment plans for chronic sufferers.
