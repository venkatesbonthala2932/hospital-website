Accepted Insurance Plans

Aadityaa Hospital is empanelled with leading Indian insurance providers. Enjoy cashless hospitalisation — no upfront payment needed for covered procedures.

info How Cashless Claims Work

1

Book Appointment & Inform TPA

Notify your insurance TPA at least 48 hours before a planned admission.

2

Pre-authorisation Letter

Hospital's insurance desk submits pre-auth to your insurer for approval.

3

Cashless Admission

Get admitted without paying the covered amount. Show your health card at the insurance desk.

4

Discharge & Final Settlement

Insurer pays the hospital directly. You pay only any non-covered or co-pay amounts.

Important Notes

  • Cashless facility is available only for procedures covered under your specific policy.
  • Pre-existing conditions may have a waiting period as per your policy terms.
  • Reimbursement claim is available for insurers not listed above — collect all bills and discharge summary.
  • For corporate group health policies, please carry your employee health card and company ID.
  • Contact our insurance desk at least 48 hours before planned admission for faster pre-authorisation.

Need Help with Your Claim?

Our dedicated insurance desk is available Monday – Saturday, 9 AM to 6 PM. We assist with pre-authorisation, claim forms, and TPA coordination.
